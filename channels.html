03 Room

She counts stock and reorders before the work stops for a missing item. Inventory has a list and an owner.

The physical room has a route: a place to arrive, a place to work, and a place to talk that is not a doorway. Surfaces stay clear enough to use. A professional room is one you can enter and start. Paper files and digital files use the same logic. A place, a name, and a person who holds the current version. The office should feel considered.

04 Customers

An inquiry leaves with a next step and a name attached. Onboarding is a sequence the new person can follow without guessing the order.

A complaint has one owner. The manner stays calm. The thread stays in one place until the matter is closed, and nobody is asked to repeat it for a second audience.
