What reaches the day.

Protect the hours that matter.

01 Gate

The calendar, the mail, travel, and the priority list are one circuit. She arranges travel around the work that has to happen in person, with the buffers written down. A request that cannot name the hour it will take does not enter the day.

The sender still gets an answer. Sometimes the answer is no. Gatekeeping is a courtesy with a spine, and the spine is what keeps the principal on the product.

02 Table

She takes a seat in some company meetings. From that seat she says what should change in the work, in the offer, and in the way attention is spent.

She interviews candidates. She gives executives feedback on performance. What leaves the room is a plain sentence someone can act on.
