AR-01 / Attention bay

Amber Roberts

A patchbay for attention. Consulting and contract work.

Protect the hours that matter.

Requests arrive hotter than a day can hold. She routes what belongs to the principal, holds the rest, and sends a plain answer either way. The product keeps the hours.

  1. 01 Gate Calendar, mail, travel, and priorities. A request names the hour it takes, or it does not pass.
  2. 02 Table A seat in some meetings. She says what should change, interviews, and gives performance feedback.
  3. 03 Room Supplies, inventory, the path through the room, and files with a current version.
  4. 04 Customers Inquiries, onboarding, and one owner for a complaint.
  5. 05 Systems Mail, scheduling, tasks. A new tool, including a new AI tool, stays out until it earns a place.
