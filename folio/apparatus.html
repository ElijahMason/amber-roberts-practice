Apparatus

What is allowed to stay.

Protect the hours that matter. She looks at tools for mail, scheduling, and tasks, and at new tools that draft, sort, or summarize.

  1. 01

    One circuit

    Mail, the schedule, and the task list should agree. She will move them when they contradict the day. She will remove a system that only exists to be maintained.

  2. 02

    A plain look

    New AI tools get the same look as any other tool. She asks what they do to the hours, not what they promise in a demonstration. Judgment stays with the practice. It is not a separate line of work.

  3. 03

    The test

    Hours return to the principal. A person still makes the decision. The office can explain the tool on a calm day. Fail any one of those, and the tool does not enter.
