Conduct

How the work is held.

Protect the hours that matter. These are the standards. The register is the work they govern.

  1. 01

    Focus

    The principal's focus is the product. Other claims on the day have to justify the hour they take.

  2. 02

    The gate

    Gatekeeping is a courtesy with a spine. An answer goes out. A refusal goes out when the request would spend the product's hours on something else.

  3. 03

    The room

    The office should feel considered. A person walking in, or opening the files, should see that the order was chosen.

  4. 04

    A complaint

    A complaint gets a calm owner. One voice. One thread. It stays there until the matter is finished.

  5. 05

    A tool

    A tool earns its place. It returns time, and a person still decides. The rest stays outside the day.
